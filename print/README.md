## Where to print
- Online: Vistaprint, BuildASign, Signs.com. Upload the PDF and choose the matching size.
- Local: FedEx Office, Staples, or a local sign shop (often same day).
- Choose "no bleed" or "fit to size"; the files are already the exact size.

## Tips
- Outdoors, coroplast and vinyl hold up to wind and rain; foam board warps when wet.
- Wind: use banners with wind slits, or weigh down an easel.
- Pair the Good Person Test poster with million dollar bills or tracts.
- Check local rules before staking signs on public property.
